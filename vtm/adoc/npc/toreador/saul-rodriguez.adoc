==== Saul Rodriguez de Santiago de Compostela
Saul is a Toreador that knows quality when he sees it in clothing, he was 
intrigued when the Ventrue PC bought leather and offered his services to 
provide them with not only the finest leathers but the best quality in 
clothing, since the Ventrue will use Quintus as his designer he is now more 
intrigued than ever to see such a reality.
