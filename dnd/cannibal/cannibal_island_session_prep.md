# Cannibal Island - Session Prep Sheets

## Session 1: Arrival & Investigation
*Character introduction, establish atmosphere, begin investigation*

### Session Goals
- Introduce characters and setting atmosphere
- Establish town NPCs and their relationships  
- Plant initial investigation seeds
- Set up caravan mission hook

### Pre-Session Prep
- [ ] Review player character backgrounds for island motivation
- [ ] Prepare ship journey roleplay prompts
- [ ] Set up town atmosphere details (sounds, smells, sights)
- [ ] Have NPC voices/mannerisms ready
- [ ] Prepare Handout #1 (Ship Rumors Table)

### Opening Scene: The Ship Journey (30 minutes)
**Setting:** 2-3 days at sea, final approach to island

#### Character Introductions
- **Prompt:** "What brought you to seek work on this remote island?"
- **Encourage:** Backgrounds that justify the expense/danger of getting here
- **Magic Users:** Require solid justification for hiding/exile

#### Returning Veteran NPC: Marcus "Dead Eye" Corwin
- **Description:** Missing left eye, nervous tics, drinks heavily
- **Personality:** Helpful but evasive, speaks in riddles
- **Key Dialogue:**
  - *"Pay's good if you live to spend it."*
  - *"Don't trust nobody who's been down in the deep shafts too long."*
  - *"Fire. Always carry fire. They don't like the light."*
- **Information:** Won't elaborate on warnings, changes subject

#### Ship's Crew Behavior
- **Dock Workers:** Efficient but avoid conversation
- **Captain:** Professional but refuses to stay overnight
- **Cargo:** Players may notice prisoner cages if they investigate (DC 12 Investigation)

### Arrival at Town (45 minutes)

#### First Impressions Scene
**Read Aloud:**
*"The ship's engine cuts to idle as you approach a rough wooden dock extending from a small coastal settlement. Forty or so buildings huddle behind a crude palisade, watchtowers rising like broken teeth against the jungle backdrop. The silence strikes you first—no market calls, no children playing, just the lap of waves and distant bird cries. The few dock workers move with purpose, avoiding eye contact as they secure the mooring lines."*

#### Key Atmosphere Details
- **Sound:** Unnatural quiet, no normal town bustle
- **Smell:** Salt air with underlying decay
- **Visual:** Fresh palisade repairs, jungle-facing watchtowers
- **People:** Minimum workers, nervous energy, quick movements

#### Meeting Governor Reynolds (15 minutes)
**Location:** Approaches dock personally (casual, not formal)
**Description:** Late 40s, weathered veteran, practical clothing
**Personality:** Friendly but tired, genuine care for newcomers

**Key Dialogue:**
- *"Welcome to our little slice of paradise. I'm Reynolds, Governor of this operation."*
- *"You'll have today to settle in, get the lay of the land. Guard duty starts tomorrow."*
- *"Ask Thomas at the armory about equipment. Beclu can help with... other necessities."*
- *"Stay inside the walls after dark. That's not a suggestion."*

**Information Given:**
- Basic guard duties and pay (100 silver/day)
- Caravan opportunities (1000 silver per mission)
- Town rules and curfew

### Town Investigation Phase (60 minutes)

#### Master of Arms Thomas Oliver
**Location:** Stone armory building
**Personality:** Professional, by-the-book, slightly paranoid
**Equipment Available:** Standard weapons/armor at normal prices
**Key Information:**
- *"Stick to regular patrols. Don't go looking for trouble."*
- *"Iron works better than steel against some things out there."*
- *"Reynolds is a good man. Keeps us alive in an impossible situation."*

#### Tradesman Beclu
**Location:** Well-maintained shop near center of town
**Personality:** Helpful merchant hiding alchemical knowledge
**Give players:** Handout #2 (Beclu's Inventory)
**Roleplay Notes:**
- Downplays alchemical skill as "good with herbs"
- Pushes certain items: antitoxin, blessed salt, healing draughts
- *"Island's got its own challenges. Best to be prepared."*
- **DC 15 Insight:** He knows more than he's letting on

#### The Well Investigation
**Description:** Left side of town, stone-lined, small altar with offerings
**Offerings:** Flowers, small carved figures, coins
**DC 12 Religion:** Protective wards against evil
**DC 15 Investigation:** Offerings are regularly refreshed
**Local Info:** *"Keeps the water pure. Can't be too careful out here."*

#### Underground Tavern (Evening Scene)
**Location:** Cool underground space, communal operation
**Atmosphere:** Veterans nursing drinks, gallows humor
**Give players:** Handout #4 (Overheard Conversations)

##### Key Veterans to Interact With:
1. **Sergeant Kane** (Retired)
   - *"Been here five years. Seen things that ain't in any manual."*
   - **DC 13 Persuasion:** Hints about mine guard behavior changes

2. **Corporal Willis** (Current)
   - *"Caravan duty's where the real money is. Also where we lose the most men."*
   - **DC 15 Intimidation:** Admits some guards "act strange" after mine shifts

3. **Private Hendricks** (Traumatized)
   - Mumbles about *"red eyes in the dark"* and *"they took Johnson alive"*
   - **DC 16 Medicine:** Showing signs of severe trauma/possible madness

### Guard Duty & Perimeter Patrol (30 minutes)

#### First Watch Assignment
**Route:** Simple patrol along palisade interior
**Partner:** Corporal Martinez (helpful, 2-year veteran)
**Observations:**
- Constant movement in jungle treeline
- Guards regularly call out "contacts" that disappear
- Palisade shows claw marks and impact damage

#### DC Checks During Patrol:
- **DC 12 Perception:** Notice shapes moving between trees
- **DC 15 Perception:** Hear whispers in unknown language
- **DC 18 Perception:** Glimpse humanoid figures watching from jungle

#### Corporal Martinez Information:
- *"They test us every night. Looking for weak spots."*
- *"Caravan duty's dangerous, but the mine... that's where it gets really weird."*
- *"Some of the mine guards, they know things they shouldn't know."*

### Session End Setup
#### Caravan Mission Hook
**Timing:** Next morning announcement
**Reynolds' Offer:** *"We've got a supply run to the mine tomorrow. Extra pay for volunteers."*
**Sergeant Introduction:** Brief meeting with Mikeal and Damien

#### Cliffhanger Elements:
- Strange sounds from jungle during night watch
- One of the mine guards acting oddly during evening
- Discovery of fresh claw marks on palisade

### Session 1 DM Notes
- **Pacing:** Let investigation be player-driven
- **Atmosphere:** Emphasize wrongness without revealing too much
- **NPCs:** Establish personalities for future sessions
- **Foreshadowing:** Plant seeds for corruption discovery

## Session 2: First Caravan Mission
*Introduce caravan mechanics, escalate danger, reveal slave operation*

### Session Goals
- Establish caravan team dynamics
- Reveal prisoner transport operation
- First major combat encounters  
- Begin corruption exposure mechanics

### Pre-Session Prep
- [ ] Review caravan route and encounter locations
- [ ] Prepare Sergeant personality voices
- [ ] Set up wave combat mechanics
- [ ] Have corruption tracking sheets ready
- [ ] Prepare Handout #5 (Jungle Encounter Signs)

### Mission Briefing (20 minutes)

#### Pre-Dawn Assembly
**Location:** Town center, ox-drawn carts being loaded
**Atmosphere:** Professional but tense, veterans checking equipment

#### Sergeant Introductions

**Sergeant Mikeal Zentu (Yellow Team Leader)**
- **Description:** Dark-skinned, calm demeanor, scimitar always ready
- **Leadership Style:** Patient teacher, defensive tactics
- **Key Dialogue:**
  - *"First rule: stay with your team. Second rule: don't be a hero."*
  - *"The jungle's alive out there. It watches, it learns, it waits."*
  - *"You'll see things today that'll make you question your choices. That's normal."*

**Sergeant Damien von Hans (Red Team Leader)**  
- **Description:** Stocky, dual axes, crossbow with unusual bolts
- **Leadership Style:** Expects competence, brotherhood bonds
- **Key Dialogue:**
  - *"We go fast, we go hard, we don't stop for anything."*
  - *"These men are your brothers now. You fight for them, they fight for you."*
  - *"Questions are for after we get back alive."*

#### Team Assignments
- **Players divided between teams** (their choice or balanced by DM)
- **Equipment check:** Final gear purchases from Beclu
- **Route briefing:** Basic path, danger zones, timing critical

#### The Prisoner Revelation
**Timing:** During cart loading
**Discovery:** DC 12 Perception to notice prisoner cages being loaded
**NPC Reactions:**
- **Mikeal:** *"Empire's expansion creates prisoners. Mine needs workers."*
- **Damien:** *"Not our job to judge. Our job is to deliver them alive."*
- **Veterans:** Matter-of-fact acceptance, some discomfort

**Player Moral Choice Point:**
- Refuse participation (consequences: loss of pay, still need to stay on island)
- Question the morality (sergeants explain "necessary evil")
- Accept professional duty (gain team respect)

### Journey to Mine (90 minutes)

#### Phase 1: Leaving Town (15 minutes)
**Atmosphere:** Tense anticipation, jungle looms ahead
**Encounter:** None, but establish ongoing threat
**Give players:** Handout #5 (Jungle Encounter Signs)

#### Phase 2: First Danger Zone - Broken Terrain (30 minutes)
**Terrain:** Rocky outcrops, steep grades, natural chokepoints
**Tactical Advantage:** Players can use high ground if smart

##### Encounter: Cannibal Scouts
- **Enemies:** 2 Undead Cannibals (probing attack)
- **Tactics:** Hit and run, test defenses
- **Purpose:** Let players see regeneration mechanics
- **NPC Reactions:** Professional response, no panic

##### Environmental Challenges:
- **DC 15 Survival:** Find safest path through broken ground
- **Success:** Avoid difficult terrain penalties
- **Failure:** Movement reduced, vulnerable to ambush

#### Phase 3: River Crossing (30 minutes)
**Description:** Wide, slow-moving river with makeshift bridge
**Tactical Disadvantage:** Single file crossing, can't retreat quickly

##### The Necromancers Choose Violence
**Trigger:** Halfway across bridge
**Encounter:** 6 Undead Cannibals + Environmental hazard

**Environmental Elements:**
- **Bridge:** 5 feet wide, 60 feet long
- **Current:** DC 15 Athletics to swim if knocked off
- **Positioning:** Cannibals attack from both sides

**Tactical Notes:**
- Sergeants coordinate defense professionally
- NPCs protect prisoners (they're valuable cargo)
- Players learn team coordination importance

#### Phase 4: Deep Mud Zone (15 minutes)
**Description:** Marshy area with patches of dangerous mud
**Navigation:** DC 15 Survival to find safe path
**Failure Consequence:** 25% chance of Giant Toad encounter

##### Mud Hazard Mechanics:
- **Detection:** DC 15 Investigation to spot dangerous areas
- **Trapped:** DC 14 Strength (Athletics) or become restrained
- **Escape:** DC 16 Strength check, pulled deeper on failure
- **Depth Levels:** 5 ft (restrained), 10 ft (unconscious), 15 ft (death)

### Arriving at Mine (30 minutes)

#### First Impressions of Mine Complex
**Read Aloud:**
*"The jungle gives way to a cleared area carved into the volcano's base. Stone buildings and mining equipment dot the complex, while the steady rhythm of picks and hammers echoes from multiple excavation sites. Guards patrol the perimeter, but unlike the nervous energy of town, these men move with disturbing confidence. Something about this place feels... wrong."*

#### Meeting the Foreman: Marcus Drummond
**Description:** Former veteran, late 50s, surprisingly welcoming
**Personality:** Grateful for supplies, knows sergeants well
**Hidden Truth:** Already corrupted, but concealing it well

**Key Dialogue:**
- *"Mikeal! Damien! Good to see friendly faces from the outside."*
- *"Fresh workers too. We'll put them to good use."*
- *"Guard rotation's been interesting lately. Some of the boys have been... dedicated."*

#### Suspicious Guard Behavior (Investigation Opportunity)
**DC 13 Perception:** Notice guards watching new prisoners intently
**DC 15 Insight:** Guards seem unusually interested in specific prisoners
**DC 17 Investigation:** Catch guard making hand signals toward jungle

#### The Mine's Unnatural Safety
**Observations for perceptive characters:**
- No jungle sounds near complex
- Animals avoid the area entirely  
- Temperature noticeably warmer underground
- **DC 16 Arcana:** Magical aura emanating from deep shafts

### Return Journey (45 minutes)

#### Changed Jungle
**Atmosphere:** More hostile, shadows seem darker
**NPC Commentary:** *"They know we've been to the mine now."*

#### Escalated Encounter: Medium Cannibal Wave
- **Enemies:** 8-10 Undead Cannibals
- **Tactics:** Coordinated assault, attempt to capture rather than kill
- **Special:** Some cannibals try to drag prisoners away

#### Environmental Challenges:
**Dusk Approaching:** Time pressure increases
**Fatigue:** Constitution saves or gain exhaustion
**Gold Weight:** Loaded carts slow movement

### Session End: First Corruption Exposure

#### That Night in Town
- **Automatic Corruption Save:** All characters make DC 12 Wisdom save
- **Success:** No effect yet, but describe subtle unease
- **Failure:** Stage 1 corruption (whispers during rest)

#### Session 2 Cliffhanger Options:
1. Player notices corruption symptoms in themselves
2. Mine guard seen meeting someone in shadows
3. Prisoner count doesn't match returning manifest
4. Strange dreams featuring red sun imagery

### Session 2 DM Notes
- **Combat Pacing:** Build from skirmish to significant threat
- **Moral Weight:** Let prisoner transport reality sink in
- **Team Dynamics:** Establish sergeant relationships
- **Corruption Tracking:** Begin careful record-keeping

## Session 3: Growing Suspicions
*Reveal mine operation details, introduce corrupted guards, escalate investigation*

### Session Goals
- Second caravan mission with adapted enemy tactics
- Discover corrupted guard activities
- Investigate mine more thoroughly
- Force moral choices about corruption

### Pre-Session Prep
- [ ] Track corruption progression from previous session
- [ ] Prepare corrupted guard stat blocks
- [ ] Set up mine investigation scenes
- [ ] Have Handout #6 (Corruption Tracker) ready
- [ ] Plan prisoner interrogation scenes

### Opening: Second Caravan Assignment (15 minutes)

#### A Changed Dynamic
**Timing:** 3 days after first mission
**Changes:** Some players may show Stage 1 corruption symptoms
**Atmosphere:** Sergeants more watchful, veterans more distant

#### Mission Briefing Differences
**Mikeal's Concerns:** *"They learned from last time. Expect different tactics."*
**Damien's Intensity:** *"Stay sharp. They'll try to divide us."*
**New Equipment:** Upgraded defenses based on previous encounter

### Enhanced Journey Challenges (75 minutes)

#### Necromancer Adaptation Phase
**Tactics:** Enemies exploit observed weaknesses from first trip
**Intelligence:** Cannibals coordinate to separate team members

#### Encounter: Divide and Conquer
- **Setup:** Cannibals create multiple smaller threats
- **Goal:** Split party between protecting prisoners and fighting
- **Enemies:** 3 groups of 3-4 Undead Cannibals each
- **Duration:** 6-8 rounds of coordinated assault

#### Advanced Environmental Challenges:
**False Paths:** DC 16 Survival to detect necromancer-created detours
**Psychological Warfare:** Whispers in player characters' native languages
**Corruption Pressure:** Additional Wisdom saves for extended exposure

### Mine Investigation Deep Dive (90 minutes)

#### Opportunity for Deeper Exploration
**Reason:** Longer supply delivery, equipment maintenance
**Time Available:** 2-3 hours while carts are unloaded/loaded
**Supervision:** Reduced due to routine nature

#### Investigation Locations:

##### 1. Slave Quarters (30 minutes)
**Access:** DC 13 Stealth to approach without guard notice
**Conditions:** Overcrowded, poor sanitation, high fear

**Key NPCs:**
- **Elena Vasquez** (Former teacher): Willing to talk if approached carefully
- **Old Henrik** (Veteran prisoner): Knows guard corruption patterns
- **Young Marcus** (Teenager): Too terrified to speak directly

**Information Available:**
- Guards take specific prisoners at night (never return)
- Strange ceremonies heard from deep mine shafts  
- Some guards "changed" after working underground
- Whispers about red light from deep tunnels

**DC 15 Persuasion with Elena:** *"They choose the strong ones, the ones who fight back. Take them down where the red light glows."*

##### 2. Guard Barracks Investigation (30 minutes)
**Challenge:** DC 16 Stealth to search without detection
**Risk:** If caught, must explain presence

**Evidence Found:**
- Personal journals with increasingly erratic entries
- Drawings of symbols matching jungle carvings
- Food stores include raw meat (unusual for guards)
- Sleep areas show signs of insomnia/restlessness

**Journal Entry Sample:** *"Day 47 - The voice grows stronger. Shows me such wonders in the deep places. Marcus still resists, but he'll understand soon. The red sun rises, and we are its children."*

##### 3. Deep Shaft Reconnaissance (30 minutes)
**Access:** Requires avoiding multiple guards and workers
**Danger:** High corruption exposure risk

**Environmental Effects:**
- Temperature increases noticeably  
- Time seems to slow (subjective feeling)
- Sounds echo strangely
- **Automatic Corruption Save:** DC 15 Constitution save

**Discoveries:**
- Strange symbols carved into tunnel walls
- Mining has been directed toward specific underground chamber
- Faint red glow emanating from deepest accessible point
- **DC 18 Arcana:** Powerful magical aura, ancient and alien

#### Confronting Corrupted Guards
**Trigger:** Investigation discovered or guards become suspicious
**Location:** Isolated area of mine complex

##### Corrupted Guard Captain: Thomas Blackwood
- **Corruption Level:** Long-term exposure (3+ months)
- **Behavior:** Attempts conversion before violence
- **Backup:** 2-3 Recently Corrupted Guards

**Conversion Attempt Dialogue:**
- *"You feel it too, don't you? The calling from below?"*
- *"Why fight for the Empire when you could serve something greater?"*
- *"The red sun offers power beyond mortal understanding."*

**Combat Triggers:**
- Players refuse conversion
- Players threaten to expose operation
- Players attempt to free prisoners

### Return Journey: Escalated Threats (60 minutes)

#### The Necromancers Respond
**Reason:** Mine disturbance detected, investigation discovered
**Escalation:** Large coordinated assault

#### Encounter: Large Cannibal Wave
- **Enemies:** 15-18 Undead Cannibals + 1 Corrupted Guard
- **Tactics:** Multi-phase assault over several rounds
- **Environment:** Use terrain defensively, protect cargo

**Phase 1:** Ranged harassment (rounds 1-2)
**Phase 2:** Flanking maneuvers (rounds 3-4)  
**Phase 3:** All-out assault (rounds 5-7)
**Phase 4:** Final desperate attempt to capture prisoners

#### NPC Sergeant Performance:
**Mikeal:** Defensive coordination, protects weakest positions
**Damien:** Aggressive counter-attack, leads by example
**Both:** Professional teamwork, cover each other's weaknesses

### Session End: Point of No Return (15 minutes)

#### Back in Town: Forced Choices
**Evidence Gathered:** Enough to know corruption exists
**Moral Decision:** What to do with information?

#### Options Presented:
1. **Report to Reynolds:** Risk if he's complicit
2. **Confront Sergeants:** Test their trustworthiness  
3. **Plan Independent Action:** Risky but maintains secrecy
4. **Attempt Escape:** Next ship in 4 days

#### Corruption Progression Check:
- **Second Exposure:** All characters make DC 13 saves
- **Mine Investigation:** Additional DC 15 save for deep shaft exposure
- **Stage 2 Symptoms:** Begin manifesting in failed saves

#### Cliffhanger Elements:
- Reynolds requests "private meeting" next morning
- Mine guards seen entering town after dark
- Prisoners from their caravan go missing overnight
- Strange dreams intensify for corrupted characters

### Session 3 DM Notes
- **Investigation Pacing:** Reward thorough exploration
- **Moral Complexity:** No clear "right" choices
- **Corruption Tracking:** Careful progression monitoring
- **Combat Scaling:** Adjust for party composition and corruption effects

## Session 4: The Truth & Final Choice
*Reveal full conspiracy, force ultimate moral decision, climactic confrontation*

### Session Goals
- Expose complete scope of conspiracy
- Present impossible moral choice
- Epic final confrontation
- Resolution of character arcs

### Pre-Session Prep
- [ ] Review all corruption tracking sheets
- [ ] Prepare final revelation scenes
- [ ] Set up temple complex encounter
- [ ] Have multiple ending scenarios ready
- [ ] Prepare emotional impact moments

### Opening: The Web Unravels (30 minutes)

#### Reynolds' "Private Meeting"
**Location:** Governor's office (stone building)
**Atmosphere:** Tense, Reynolds more guarded than usual
**Truth:** He knows players have been investigating

**Reynolds' Revelation:**
- *"You've been asking questions. Digging where you shouldn't."*
- *"I know what you found at the mine. What you think you understand."*
- *"The question is: what are you going to do about it?"*

#### The Governor's Truth:
**Partial Honesty:** Admits to knowing about corruption
**Justification:** *"We feed a few dozen prisoners to keep hundreds alive."*
**The Bigger Picture:** *"You think this is evil? Wait until you see what happens if we don't cooperate."*

#### Critical Information Revealed:
- Cooperation with necromancers is deliberate
- Alternative is total annihilation of island population
- The Anchor grows stronger, will eventually break free
- Empire is unaware of true situation

### The Sergeants' Choice (45 minutes)

#### Private Meeting with Mikeal and Damien
**Location:** Underground tavern, after hours
**Atmosphere:** Two soldiers who've reached their limit

**Mikeal's Position:**
- *"We've suspected for months. Been gathering our own evidence."*
- *"Can't fight an enemy we don't understand."*
- *"Four years of this. We're done being complicit."*

**Damien's Stance:**
- *"Our men deserve better than being sacrificed to monsters."*
- *"Empire trained us to fight enemies, not feed them."*
- *"You want to do something about this, we're with you."*

#### Alliance Formation:
**Resources Offered:** 
- Sergeant expertise and leadership
- Loyal soldiers (8-10 men unaware of full truth)
- Knowledge of island layout and enemy patterns
- Tactical coordination for any assault

**Plans Discussed:**
1. **Expose Conspiracy:** Send evidence to Empire (risky communication)
2. **Destroy the Anchor:** Assault temple complex
3. **Escape with Evidence:** Get off island, return with help
4. **Negotiate with Enemy:** Attempt to understand their goals

### The Final Investigation: Temple Approach (60 minutes)

#### Journey to Temple Complex
**Route:** Deep jungle, following corrupted guard tracks
**Distance:** 8 kilometers through hostile territory
**Composition:** Players + 2 Sergeants + 6-8 loyal soldiers

#### Enhanced Environmental Challenges:
**Corruption Intensity:** DC 16 saves every hour
**Psychological Pressure:** Whispers intensify, false visions
**Physical Manifestations:** Reality distortions near temple

#### Encounter: Temple Guardians
- **Enemies:** 12 Undead Cannibals + 3 Long-term Corrupted Guards
- **Tactics:** Coordinated defense of sacred ground
- **Environment:** Use temple architecture and jungle cover

### The Heart of Darkness: Temple Complex (75 minutes)

#### Approaching the Temple
**Read Aloud:**
*"The jungle opens into an ancient clearing dominated by a stone temple that predates any human presence on this island. Carved totems ring the complex, their alien geometries seeming to shift when viewed peripherally. The air thrums with unnatural energy, and at the temple's heart, a red glow pulses like a cosmic heartbeat. This is the source. This is where it all began."*

#### Temple Layout Encounters:

##### Outer Ring: Ritual Circles (15 minutes)
- **Enemies:** 6-8 Undead Cannibals + sacrificial victims
- **Discovery:** Active ritual in progress
- **Choice:** Interrupt (alerting all enemies) or observe (gain tactical intel)

##### Middle Ring: Avatar's Approach (30 minutes)
**The Revelation of Nilith El'Zen:**
- **Appearance:** Six-armed humanoid, eyeless, hook-mouth
- **Communication:** Telepathic contact with all characters
- **Offers:** Personal temptations based on character backgrounds

**Avatar's Temptations (Individual):**
- **Fighter:** *"Unmatched strength, victory in every battle"*
- **Rogue:** *"Secrets of the universe, power over life and death"*
- **Cleric:** *"Your god's true power, divine authority made manifest"*
- **Wizard:** *"Knowledge beyond mortal comprehension, magic unlimited"*

**Corruption Saves:** DC 18 Wisdom saves for all characters
**Failure Effects:** Immediate advancement to next corruption stage

##### Inner Sanctum: The Anchor Chamber (30 minutes)

#### The Anchor Revealed
**Description:** Massive crystalline formation, blindingly radiant
**Effects:** 
- **DC 18 Constitution save:** Avoid blindness from radiance
- **Telepathic Contact:** Direct communication with cosmic entity
- **Reality Distortion:** 60-foot radius of warped space-time

**The Anchor's Truth:**
- Ancient cosmic entity imprisoned in crystal form
- Growing stronger through life essence sacrifices
- Will eventually break free to consume reality
- Mine excavation will reach it in 2-3 years

#### The Ultimate Choice

**Option 1: Destroy the Anchor**
**Requirements:** Coordinated assault on crystal structure
**Immediate Effect:** Island-wide cataclysm begins
**Consequence:** Everyone on island has 10 minutes to live
**Moral Weight:** Sacrifice hundreds to save millions/billions

**Option 2: Contain the Anchor**
**Requirements:** Complex ritual using party member's life force
**Immediate Effect:** Delays entity's emergence by decades
**Consequence:** Volunteer becomes permanent guardian (effective death)
**Moral Weight:** Individual sacrifice for temporary solution

**Option 3: Escape and Warn Empire**
**Requirements:** Fighting retreat to coast
**Immediate Effect:** Corruption spreads unchecked
**Consequence:** Entity grows stronger, eventual global catastrophe
**Moral Weight:** Abandoning immediate victims for larger warning

**Option 4: Submit to Corruption**
**Requirements:** Accept Anchor's influence willingly
**Immediate Effect:** Gain supernatural power
**Consequence:** Become agents of cosmic horror
**Moral Weight:** Personal survival through betrayal of humanity

### Final Battle: Whatever Choice Is Made (45 minutes)

#### If Players Choose to Fight:
**Enemy Forces:**
- Nilith El'Zen Avatar (full stats)
- 20+ Undead Cannibals (arriving in waves)
- 4-6 Long-term Corrupted Guards
- Environmental hazards and reality distortions

**Phases:**
1. **Avatar Alone:** Tests party capabilities
2. **First Wave:** 8 Cannibals join fight
3. **Corrupted Guards:** Tactical coordination
4. **Overwhelming Force:** Remaining cannibals arrive
5. **Desperation:** Avatar uses ultimate abilities

#### If Players Choose Destruction:
**Sequence:**
1. **Anchor Assault:** Multi-round destruction process
2. **Reality Collapse:** Island begins sinking
3. **Evacuation Race:** 10 rounds to reach boats
4. **Final Sacrifice:** NPC/PC heroic moments

#### If Players Choose Escape:
**Sequence:**
1. **Fighting Retreat:** Running battle through jungle
2. **Town Evacuation:** Convince others to flee
3. **Naval Escape:** Commandeer available vessels
4. **Long-term Consequences:** Setting up future campaigns

### Resolution: Multiple Endings (30 minutes)

#### Heroic Sacrifice Ending
- Anchor destroyed, island collapses
- Survivors escape with warning for Empire
- Characters' names remembered as heroes
- **Corruption:** All corruption effects end

#### Guardian Ending  
- Anchor contained, volunteer becomes eternal guardian
- Island operations continue under new management
- Corruption reduced but not eliminated
- **Future:** Setup for return campaign

#### Warning Ending
- Evidence delivered to Empire
- Larger military response organized
- Characters as advisors for future assault
- **Consequences:** Time pressure for next attempt

#### Corruption Ending
- Characters join cosmic horror conspiracy
- Become NPCs under DM control
- **Future:** Potential antagonists for new campaign
- **Corruption:** Complete transformation

### Session 4 DM Notes
- **Player Agency:** Honor whatever choice they make
- **Emotional Impact:** Focus on character relationships
- **Epic Scale:** Make final battle feel climactic
- **Consequences:** Show long-term effects of decisions
- **Character Arcs:** Provide satisfying conclusions

## Quick Reference: Common DCs & Mechanics

### Investigation DCs
- **Easy Information:** DC 10 (obvious clues)
- **Moderate Secrets:** DC 15 (require effort)
- **Hidden Truth:** DC 20 (dangerous knowledge)

### Corruption Save DCs
- **Week 1-2:** DC 12
- **Week 3-4:** DC 15
- **Week 5+:** DC 18
- **Direct Contact:** +5 to DC

### Combat Scaling
- **3 Players:** -25% enemies
- **4-5 Players:** Use as written
- **6+ Players:** +25% enemies or +1 CR

### NPC Attitude Tracking
- **Friendly:** Helpful, shares information freely
- **Neutral:** Professional, basic cooperation
- **Unfriendly:** Suspicious, minimal help
- **Hostile:** Actively opposes players
