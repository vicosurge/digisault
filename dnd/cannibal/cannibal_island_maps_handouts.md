### Handout #3: Anchor Corruption Warning Signs
*For DM reference - describe symptoms as they develop*

#### Physical Signs
- Eyes develop faint luminescence in darkness
- Skin becomes noticeably cooler to touch
- Fingernails grow faster, become sharper
- Voice occasionally harmonizes with itself
- Shadows seem slightly wrong or delayed

#### Behavioral Changes
- Increased interest in the mine's operations
- Defensive when questioned about guard activities
- Uses "we" instead of "I" in conversation
- Shows unusual knowledge of things happening elsewhere
- Becomes agitated when away from the island

#### Supernatural Symptoms
- Knows things they shouldn't know
- Can sense other corrupted individuals
- Occasionally speaks in Deep Speech
- Dreams of a red sun with writhing tentacles
- Hears whispers in ancient languages

### Handout #5: Jungle Encounter Signs
*Environmental clues for building tension*

#### Audio Cues
- Rustling that stops the moment characters look toward it
- Animal calls that echo from impossible directions  
- Whispers in languages that sound almost familiar
- Rhythmic tapping that might be drums... or might not

#### Visual Indicators
- Trees with symbols carved deep into bark
- Paths that seem to shift when not directly observed
- Smoke rising from areas with no visible fire
- Shapes moving between trees at the edge of vision

#### Physical Evidence
- Gnawed bones arranged in deliberate patterns
- Offerings left at the base of certain trees
- Tracks that are almost human, but wrong somehow
- Areas where plants grow in unnatural spirals

## Investigation Clue Progression

### Session 1 Clues (Subtle Hints)
- Town unusually quiet for its size
- Fresh repairs on palisade suggest recent attacks
- Veterans in tavern avoid certain topics
- Well has protective offerings (suggest supernatural threat)
- No children or elderly visible in town

### Session 2 Clues (Growing Evidence)  
- Prisoner transport cages on caravan
- Guards act nervous around certain mine personnel
- Jungle attack patterns show intelligence
- Undead cannibals avoid certain areas
- Mine produces more gold than geology suggests

### Session 3 Clues (Damning Evidence)
- Guard caught transferring prisoners
- Corruption symptoms in mine personnel
- Ancient symbols match those found in jungle
- Time distortion effects near mine depths
- Corrupted guards share information impossibly

### Session 4 Clues (Full Revelation)
- Anchor's presence revealed through investigation
- Nilith El'Zen communications intercepted
- Full scope of conspiracy uncovered
- Reynolds and foreman's complicity exposed
- True purpose of island operation revealed

## Quick Reference Tables

### Random Jungle Encounters (Roll 1d12)
1-3: Probing cannibals (1-2 scouts)
4-6: Environmental hazard (mud, heat, difficult terrain)  
7-8: False alarm (natural wildlife sounds)
9-10: Ambush site (evidence of previous attack)
11: Medium cannibal group (4-6 creatures)
12: Supernatural phenomenon (corruption effect)

### Guard Patrol Schedule
- **Dawn:** Shift change, vulnerability window
- **Morning:** Heavy patrols around mine entrance
- **Midday:** Reduced presence (heat shelter)
- **Afternoon:** Caravan prep activities
- **Dusk:** Perimeter security focus
- **Night:** Corrupted guards more active

### Corruption Exposure Frequency
- **Light Exposure:** Once per week
- **Moderate Exposure:** Once per day (mine workers)
- **Heavy Exposure:** Multiple times per day (corrupted guards)
- **Extreme Exposure:** Continuous (direct Anchor contact)
