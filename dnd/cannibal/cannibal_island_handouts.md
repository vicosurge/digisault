# Cannibal Island: Handouts and Maps

*Handouts 1–4 are for the players. Handout 5 and the maps are for the DM.*

## Handout 4: Jungle Signs

*Sessions 1–3, on the road. Drop one in whenever the tension sags.*

- Rustling that stops the moment someone looks toward it.
- Animal calls that echo from impossible directions.
- Whispers in a language that sounds almost familiar.
- Rhythmic tapping that might be drums, or might not.
- Symbols carved deep into the bark of trees.
- Paths that seem to shift when nobody is looking at them.
- Smoke rising where there's no fire.
- Gnawed bones laid out in careful patterns.
- Tracks that are almost human.
- Plants growing in spirals.

## Maps

### The Town

```
        N
        ↑
   [Watchtower]            [Watchtower]
   ═══════════════════════════════════   ← palisade (fresh repairs)
   ║  1 Governor's house   2 Armory    ║
   ║                                   ║
   ║  3 Well + altar     5 Beclu       ║
   ║                                   ║
   ║        4 Underground tavern       ║
   ║   (wooden houses and workshops)   ║
   ═══════════════════════════════════
   [Watchtower]   6 Docks   [Watchtower]
                    ~~~~
```

1. **Governor's house** (stone): office, cells and Reynolds's quarters.
2. **Armory** (stone).
3. **Well**: fresh water, and an altar of offerings.
4. **Underground tavern**: cool and dim, where the veterans drink.
5. **Beclu's shop**.
6. **Docks**: room for two medium ships.

The six watchtowers are 15 feet tall: two face the jungle, two the docks, and two the flanks.

### The Road and the Island

```
            ▲ Volcano
     Mine ■ │ ╲
            │   ╲  Temple (on the jungle slope, 8 km off the road)
      mud   ░     ╲
            │
      river ≈≈≈≈ (the bridge)
            │
  broken    ▒
  ground    │
            ■ Town
          ~~~~~~ sea
```

The road is about 25 km one way, which takes all day, dawn to dusk. It passes three danger spots: broken ground, the river bridge and the mud. There are no safe places outside the walls.

### The Mine

```
          Volcano
   ┌───────────────────────┐
   │  open pits  ⚒  ⚒  ⚒   │
   │  foreman's office      │
   │  guard barracks        │
   │  slave quarters  ⛓     │
   │  deep shaft ↓↓↓        │ → toward the Anchor
   └────────┬──────────────┘
          main gate
```

The mine has five permanent guards and rotating patrols. The deep shaft leaves the gold veins and heads straight down toward a red glow.

### The Temple

```
       Anchor chamber (bottom of the stair)
                 ▼
   ┌─────────────────────────┐
   │   middle ring: Nilith    │
   │                          │
   │ outer ring: ritual       │
   │ circles, altars, totems  │
   └─────────────────────────┘
        jungle approaches
```

The temple has raised platforms, narrow passages between the ritual areas, fire pits, and totems to take cover behind. The stair from the middle ring descends into the volcano, to the Anchor.
