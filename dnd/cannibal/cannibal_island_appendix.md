# Cannibal Island - Appendix

## How to Use This Adventure

Cannibal Island is a 3–4 session horror adventure for 4–5 characters of 3rd–5th level. It is designed to be run as a standalone module or as an arc within a larger campaign.

**What the DM Needs.** The three companion documents cover the campaign overview, scene-by-scene session prep, and maps with player handouts. This appendix provides everything mechanical: the Corruption subsystem, stat blocks for all named enemies and allies, and new equipment. All DC references in the session prep sheets correspond to the mechanics defined here.

**Tone Guidance.** This adventure leans on psychological dread over gore. The horror works best when players feel the conspiracy closing around them before they understand its full shape. Corruption should feel like a slow tide, not a sudden punishment. Reward players who investigate; make all four final choices feel genuinely costly.

**Content Warning.** This adventure depicts colonial violence, forced labor, and body horror. Discuss expectations with your table before play.

## New Mechanic: The Corruption of the Anchor

The Anchor is an ancient cosmic entity imprisoned in crystalline form deep beneath the island. Its psychic influence seeps outward constantly, bending the minds of those who linger nearby. Corruption is the mechanical representation of this influence. Track it for each character independently.

### Exposure Events

Each time a character experiences a triggering event, they make a saving throw. On a failure, they advance one corruption stage.

| Trigger | Save | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| First night's rest on the island | DC 10 Wisdom | One-time only |
| Each subsequent week on the island | DC 12 Constitution | Cumulative pressure |
| Witnessing an active sacrifice ritual | DC 14 Wisdom | Requires direct observation |
| Extended contact with a Corruption Aura | See creature stat block | Varies by creature |
| Entering the deep mine shaft | DC 15 Constitution | Each visit |
| Physical contact with The Anchor | DC 18 Constitution | Immediate |
| Conversation of 10+ minutes with a Stage 4–5 creature | DC 14 Wisdom | Must be willing conversation |
| Failing a save against Nilith El'Zen's Eldritch Presence | Automatic | See stat block |

### Corruption Stages

**Stage 0 — Unexposed.** No effects.

**Stage 1 — Whispers.** The Anchor's presence edges into dreams.
- *Symptoms:* Vivid nightmares of a pulsing red sun; whispers in unknown languages during moments of quiet.
- *Mechanical Effect:* Disadvantage on Wisdom (Insight) checks. At the end of each long rest, make a DC 12 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, the rest provides no benefit — no hit points recovered, no spell slots regained.
- *Visible Signs (for DM use):* Eyes occasionally catch light strangely in darkness.

**Stage 2 — Touched.** The Anchor takes root.
- *Symptoms:* Speaks in Deep Speech during sleep without knowing it; an uncomfortable interest in what happens at the mine.
- *Mechanical Effect:* All Stage 1 effects persist. The character can passively sense corrupted creatures within 30 feet (no action required). All Wisdom saving throws are made at disadvantage.
- *Visible Signs:* Skin is noticeably cool to the touch. Fingernails grow faster and become faintly sharp.

**Stage 3 — Claimed.** The Anchor's will begins to override personal agency.
- *Symptoms:* A compulsive instinct to protect the conspiracy and its servants, even against the character's better judgment.
- *Mechanical Effect:* All Stage 2 effects persist. When the character would take a hostile action against a corrupted creature of CR 1 or higher, they must first succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or the action is wasted and the turn ends. The character gains darkvision 60 ft. (or extends existing darkvision by 60 ft.) and their Constitution score increases by 2 (maximum 20) as the Anchor reinforces the body.
- *Visible Signs:* Voice occasionally harmonizes with itself on certain words. Shadows appear fractionally delayed.

**Stage 4 — Consumed.** The Anchor's will dominates.
- *Symptoms:* The character's personality fragments. Their priorities shift toward the conspiracy's goals without conscious awareness.
- *Mechanical Effect:* All Stage 3 effects persist. Once per day, at any point, the DM may require the character to make a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, the DM controls the character's next action (the character is unaware this is happening — frame it as impulse, not possession). The character also gains a +2 bonus to attack rolls and saving throws as the Anchor empowers its vessel.
- *Visible Signs:* Eyes glow faintly crimson in darkness. Occasionally speaks fragmented Deep Speech aloud while awake.

**Stage 5 — Vessel.** The character is lost.
- The character becomes an NPC under the DM's full control. They retain all class features, equipment, and memories — but serve the Anchor's will above all else. They are not hostile toward former allies unless those allies threaten the Anchor. They may even be genuinely distressed by what they are doing. See *Reducing Corruption* below for the only paths back.
- *Visible Signs:* A deep red glow behind the eyes, visible even in daylight. Voice carries an unnatural resonance.

### Reducing Corruption

- **Greater Restoration.** Reduces one creature's corruption stage by 1. Can only be applied once per long rest per target.
- **Beclu's Suppression Compound.** Allows the target to immediately make an additional saving throw against the most recent exposure that advanced their stage, made with advantage. Does not reduce existing stages on its own.
- **Distance.** A character who spends 7 uninterrupted days more than 5 miles from the Anchor may make a DC 12 Constitution saving throw at the end of that period. On a success, reduce corruption by 1 stage.
- **Destroying the Anchor.** Immediately reduces all corrupted creatures to Stage 0, except those at Stage 5. Stage 5 creatures are reduced to Stage 3 and must pursue conventional treatment from there.
- **Wish.** Reduces any number of stages for one target. The only reliable cure for Stage 5.

## The Anchor

*Artifact*

The Anchor is a crystalline formation of unearthly origin, roughly 30 feet tall and 20 feet in diameter at its base. It predates all human presence on the island and all recorded history of the Empire. It cannot be moved, dismantled, or damaged by conventional means. Up close, it radiates warmth and an almost musical vibration. It is beautiful. It speaks directly to the mind, and what it says feels true.

**Psychic Broadcast.** The Anchor continuously broadcasts psychic energy within a 1-mile radius. All living creatures in this radius are subject to corruption exposure saves (see table above). The Anchor is the source of all corruption on the island.

**Preservation Field.** Undead and corrupted creatures within 300 feet of the Anchor have advantage on all saving throws, gain 5 hit points at the start of each of their turns, and are under the effect of a permanent *sanctuary* spell (DC 15 Wisdom save to target them).

**Seductive Radiance.** Any creature that looks directly at the Anchor must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or become charmed by it for 1 hour, unable to take any action that would damage the Anchor or move more than 60 feet away from it.

**Destroying the Anchor.** The Anchor cannot be harmed by conventional weapons or most magic. It has 300 hit points and immunity to all damage except radiant. If three or more creatures strike the Anchor simultaneously in the same round (each dealing any amount of damage), the Anchor also takes 10 radiant damage regardless of damage type.

When the Anchor is reduced to 0 hit points, it detonates. All creatures within 600 feet must make a DC 20 Dexterity saving throw, taking 120 (16d12+20) radiant damage on a failure, or half as much on a success. The detonation destabilizes the island's geology. It begins collapsing into the ocean over the next 10 rounds (1 minute). Creatures who escape the island by boat before that time survive.

## Bestiary

### Monster Lore

**Undead Cannibals.** These are not raised skeletons or mindless zombies — they are the island's original jungle tribes, partially animated by the Anchor's overflow energy and bound to the necromancers' will. They retain enough cognition to set ambushes, learn from previous encounters, and communicate in fragmented bursts of their original language. Fire disrupts the Anchor's animating energy and prevents their regeneration. The necromancers did not create this — they discovered it and weaponized it. The tribes were already changing long before the Empire arrived.

**Corrupted Guards.** Corruption does not make people evil — it makes them certain. The guards believe entirely that what they are doing is necessary, that the Anchor's promises are real, and that anyone who opposes them is the true threat. They are not cackling villains. Many of them were decent soldiers. The higher the corruption stage, the less of the original person remains — but traces never fully disappear.

**Nilith El'Zen.** The Anchor's avatar is not its true form — the true form is the crystal itself, the imprisoned entity too vast and alien to manifest physically. The avatar is a projection, a mouth through which it speaks and extends its will. Nilith El'Zen does not want to destroy the world. It wants to consume it — slowly, completely, and joyfully, the way something ancient and hungry consumes a meal. When it speaks to characters, it is not lying about what it offers them. It can deliver on every promise. The price is simply everything they are.

*Large aberration, chaotic evil*

*Final boss. Session 4.*

Nilith El'Zen is not encountered in combat without warning. Before the players ever reach the temple, they should have heard its voice in their heads, seen its silhouette at the edge of visions, and had one or two characters tempted by its offers. When it finally appears physically, it should feel like something they already know.

**Whisper of Madness (Costs 2 Actions).** Nilith targets one creature within 60 feet. That creature must succeed on a DC 18 Wisdom saving throw or use its reaction to make one weapon attack against the nearest ally.

**Manifest Horror (Costs 3 Actions).** Nilith channels the Anchor's full presence. Every non-corrupted creature within 60 feet must succeed on a DC 18 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened and advance one corruption stage. On a success, the creature is immune to Manifest Horror for 24 hours.

## New Equipment

All prices are in silver pieces (sp). Items listed in Beclu's inventory in the session prep sheets use the mechanics below.

**Healing Draught.** *Potion, common.* When you drink this, you regain 2d4 + 2 hit points. (25 sp)

**Antitoxin Vial.** *Adventuring gear.* When you drink this, you have advantage on saving throws against poison for 1 hour. (15 sp)

**Fever Break Tonic.** *Potion, common.* For 8 hours after drinking, you have advantage on saving throws against disease and the poisoned condition. (20 sp)

**Jungle Sight Drops.** *Potion, uncommon.* Applying these to your eyes gives you darkvision out to 30 feet for 1 hour. If you already have darkvision, its range extends by 30 feet instead. (30 sp)

**Courage Elixir.** *Potion, uncommon.* For 10 minutes after drinking, you have advantage on saving throws against the frightened condition and against the Anchor's Psychic Broadcast corruption saves. (35 sp)

**Stamina Boost.** *Potion, uncommon.* For 4 hours after drinking, you ignore one level of exhaustion. You cannot benefit from this elixir again until you complete a long rest. (40 sp)

**Jungle Spider Venom (10 doses).** *Poison.* A creature that takes piercing or slashing damage from a weapon coated in this poison must succeed on a DC 14 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned for 1 minute. The creature repeats this save at the end of each of its turns. While poisoned, it has disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks. Applying the venom takes an action. (25 sp per pack of 10 arrows or bolts; 8 sp per individual weapon dose)

**Blessed Salt (1 lb.).** *Wondrous item, common.* When scattered in a 10-foot line or ring, undead creatures must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw to cross or enter the barrier. The salt loses potency after 1 hour or if exposed to running water. (10 sp per pound)

**Spider Silk Rope (50 ft.).** *Adventuring gear.* This lightweight rope grants advantage on ability checks made to climb using it. Its breaking strength is equivalent to hempen rope. (15 sp)

**Jungle Cloak.** *Wondrous item, common.* While wearing this cloak in natural terrain (jungle, forest, swamp), you gain a +2 bonus to Dexterity (Stealth) checks. (30 sp)

**Beclu's Suppression Compound.** *Potion, rare. Not for sale.* Beclu provides 1–2 doses at his discretion, typically after players earn his trust (Session 2 or 3 onward). When administered to a corrupted creature, that creature immediately makes an additional saving throw against the most recent corruption exposure that advanced its stage, made with advantage. On a success, its corruption stage decreases by 1. A creature can only benefit from this compound once per long rest.
