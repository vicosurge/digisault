# Cannibal Island Campaign Guide
*A 3-4 Session D&D 5e Horror Adventure*

## Campaign Overview

**Theme:** Colonial horror with cosmic undertones, moral ambiguity, and the price of survival
**Tone:** Psychological horror building to overwhelming action, moral choices with no clean answers
**Magic Rarity:** Extremely rare (1 in 250,000), practitioners hide among population
**Player Level:** Recommended 3rd-5th level characters

## Setting: The Empire of Dragons

A 60km diameter jungle island serving as a remote mining operation for the expanding Empire. What appears to be a simple gold mining venture masks a horrifying conspiracy involving necromancers, an ancient cosmic entity, and systematic human sacrifice.

## Session Structure

### Session 1: Arrival & First Impressions
**Goals:** Character introduction, establish atmosphere, initial investigation

#### The Ship Journey (Opening)
- **Duration:** 2-3 days at sea
- **Character Bonding:** Players share backgrounds and motivations
- **Foreshadowing:** 
  - Returning veteran on ship gives cryptic warnings
  - Players may notice chained prisoners in ship's hold
  - Sailors share unsettling rumors about jungle drums

#### Arrival at the Island
- **Atmosphere:** Unnatural silence, minimal dock activity, sense of watchful dread
- **Visual Cues:** Hasty palisade repairs, watchtowers facing jungle (not sea), townspeople avoid eye contact
- **The Smell:** Underlying decay despite ocean breeze

#### Key NPCs - First Meetings

**Captain Governor Reynolds**
- Casual greeting of new arrivals (not formal office meeting)
- Lives in only stone structure in town
- Secretly complicit in the conspiracy

**Tradesman Beclu** 
- Appears to be skilled herbalist, actually an alchemist in hiding
- Sells elixirs, poisoned arrows, specialized gear
- Fair pricing but expensive goods
- Motivated by earning court position back home
- Invested in everyone's survival

**The Well**
- Left side of village, source of fresh water
- Small altar with offerings nearby
- Symbol of hope and purity for residents
- Must be kept "clean and away from evil"

**Underground Tavern**
- Communal space, cooler refuge from heat
- Veterans gather here with gallows humor
- Information varies by NPC:
  - Some share tactical warnings
  - Others give too many horrific details
  - All show signs of psychological trauma

#### First Day Activities
- **Guard Duty Assignment:** Learn patrol routes, basic town layout
- **Investigation Opportunities:** Explore town, meet NPCs, gather rumors
- **Jungle Watching:** Guards regularly call out "contacts" that disappear
- **The Testing:** Things in jungle constantly probe defenses

### Session 2: First Caravan Mission
**Goals:** Introduce caravan mechanics, escalate danger, reveal slave operation

#### Mission Briefing
- **Pay Scale:** 1000 silver for caravan duty (10x guard pay)
- **Timing:** Critical to complete journey in one day (dawn to dusk)
- **Team Composition:** 20 soldiers (10 Yellow/Mikeal, 10 Red/Damien) plus player characters

#### The Sergeants

**Sergeant Mikeal Zentu (Yellow/Blue Team)**
- **Role:** Defensive tactics, fast movement over heavy armor
- **Personality:** Patient teacher, willing to mentor newcomers
- **Combat Style:** Iaijutsu (quick-draw) scimitar techniques
- **Worldview:** Sees slave operation as necessary evil for Empire
- **Goal:** Start mercenary company with Damien after island service

**Sergeant Damien von Hans (Red Team)**
- **Role:** Offensive operations, front-line assault
- **Personality:** Treats soldiers as brotherhood, expects competence
- **Combat Style:** Dual +2 axes, poisoned crossbow bolts (jungle spider venom)
- **Worldview:** Professional indifference to moral complexities
- **Teaching Style:** Learn on battlefield or die
- **United Front:** Always supports Mikeal publicly

#### Caravan Composition
- **Vehicles:** Large ox-drawn carts
- **Cargo Out:** Food, water, medicine, tools, prisoner cells with slaves
- **Cargo Return:** Carts loaded with gold
- **Prisoners:** War captives from Empire's expansion, some criminals/debtors

#### Route Hazards
- **River Crossing:** Necromancers choose when to make deadly
- **Deep Mud Zones:** Giant toads lurk beneath, oxen have been swallowed whole
- **Broken Terrain:** Disadvantages caravan, but skilled players can use defensively
- **Constant Surveillance:** Always being watched and tested

#### First Trip Encounters
- **Psychological Warfare:** 
  - Whispers in unknown languages
  - Animal cries of pain/distress
  - Shadows moving just outside vision
- **Probing Attacks:** Medium-sized groups test defenses
- **The River:** Crossing made dangerous at necromancers' discretion

### Session 3: The Mine & Growing Suspicions
**Goals:** Reveal mine operation, introduce corrupted guards, escalate investigation

#### The Mine Complex
- **Size:** 100+ people, operates day and night
- **Status:** Fortress-like, jungle threats won't approach it
- **Leadership:** Foreman doubles as military commander, 5 permanent guards
- **Workers:** All slaves with numbered days
- **Protection Mystery:** Why doesn't jungle attack here?

#### The Foreman
- **Background:** Former veteran who worked his way up
- **Relationship:** Knows both sergeants well, grateful for supplies
- **Corruption Status:** Secretly complicit with necromancers
- **Suspicious Guards:** Some act erratically in his absence

#### Second Caravan Trip - Escalation
- **Necromancer Adaptation:** Exploit observed weaknesses, divide groups
- **New Tactics:** Different undead types, evolved strategies
- **Corrupted Guard Behavior:**
  - Erratic actions blamed on "mine stress"
  - Caught in suspicious activities
  - Secret prisoner handoffs to necromancers

#### Investigation Opportunities
- **Jungle Clues:**
  - Sacrificial sites with ritual circles
  - Ancient deity symbols carved in trees
  - Tracks leading to/from mine during guard meetings
- **Environmental Anomalies:**
  - Animals behave erratically near mine
  - Time feels slower underground
  - Strange heat emanates from deep levels
- **Prisoner Interviews:**
  - Most slaves too terrified to speak
  - Clever questioning reveals guard corruption
  - Stories of missing prisoners

#### Guard Confrontation
- **Discovery:** Players witness prisoner transfer or instruction meetings
- **Timing:** When foreman distracted or absent
- **Response Escalation:**
  1. Attempt to convert players to cause
  2. Fight to death if conversion fails
  3. Call other corrupted guards if possible

### Session 4: The Truth & Final Choice
**Goals:** Reveal full conspiracy, force ultimate moral decision

#### The Complete Picture
- **The Anchor:** Ancient crystalline artifact deep in mine
- **True Purpose:** Slowly being excavated, will be reached in years
- **Corruption Source:** Provides necromancers' power, protects mine
- **Description:** Blindingly bright crystal, warm to touch, telepathically seductive
- **Effect:** Speaks directly to minds, corrupts based on willpower
- **Promises:** Offers each person their deepest desires

#### Corruption Mechanics
- **Timeframe:** Hours to months depending on individual willpower
- **Player Exposure:** Will saves during sleep periods near mine
- **NPC Complicity:** Reynolds and foreman already corrupted
- **The Real Conspiracy:** Entire operation feeds necromancers while slowly approaching artifact

#### The Sergeants' Choice
- **Their Suspicions:** Have theories but lack proof
- **Alliance Potential:** Will join players if presented with evidence
- **Their Goal:** Still want mercenary company, but won't ignore cosmic horror

#### Final Revelation: The Jungle Temple
- **Location:** Heart of jungle, largest cannibal tribe's base
- **The Avatar:** Nilith El'Zen manifestation
  - Thin humanoid, six arms in different mudras
  - Eyeless head with hook-extended mouth
  - Red sun tattoo on chest (god's true form)
  - Promises necromancers power for life essence sacrifices

#### The Ultimate Choice

**Option 1: Destroy the Anchor**
- **Consequence:** Entire island collapses into ocean
- **Casualties:** Everyone on island dies
- **Benefit:** Prevents cosmic horror from spreading to world

**Option 2: Escape**
- **Method:** Next ship (not part of conspiracy)
- **Consequence:** Horror continues to grow
- **Moral Weight:** Abandoning slaves and future victims

**Option 3: Join the Conspiracy**
- **Temptation:** Anchor offers personal desires
- **Corruption:** Gradual loss of humanity
- **Power:** Access to necromantic abilities

**Option 4: Fight Without Destroying**
- **Challenge:** Defeat avatar and necromancers
- **Risk:** Anchor remains, corruption spreads
- **Escalation:** Avatar sends waves of undead cannibals
- **Time Pressure:** Longer fight = more townspeople corrupted

#### Final Battle Mechanics
- **Location:** Jungle temple complex
- **Enemy Forces:** 50-100 undead cannibals in waves
- **The Avatar:** Nilith El'Zen with reality-warping abilities
- **Escalation:** Town corruption spreads based on battle duration
- **Environmental:** Use temple architecture and jungle terrain

## Key NPCs Summary

**Captain Governor Reynolds:** Island commander, secretly corrupted
**Tradesman Beclu:** Hidden alchemist seeking court position
**Sergeant Mikeal Zentu:** Patient teacher, defensive tactics specialist
**Sergeant Damien von Hans:** Brotherhood leader, offensive specialist
**The Foreman:** Mine commander, complicit with conspiracy
**Corrupted Guards:** Various stages of anchor influence
**Nilith El'Zen:** Avatar of ancient cosmic entity

## Atmosphere & Horror Elements

**Psychological Horror:**
- Constant sense of being watched
- Gradual revelation of conspiracy scope
- Moral complicity in slave operation
- Corruption of trusted figures

**Environmental Horror:**
- Jungle as living, malevolent entity
- Underground heat and time distortion
- Unnatural silence and behavior
- Physical decay masking spiritual rot

**Cosmic Horror:**
- Ancient entity beyond human comprehension
- Reality-warping artifact
- Inevitable doom vs. unthinkable sacrifice
- Knowledge that drives people mad

## Magic in the Setting

**Rarity:** 1 in 250,000 people
**Social Status:** Hunted and persecuted historically
**Hiding:** Practitioners blend into normal population
**Power Level:** When revealed, extremely potent
**Items:** Similarly rare and valuable
**Justification:** Players need compelling reasons for magical abilities

## Campaign Themes

**Moral Ambiguity:** No clean choices, all options have terrible costs
**Survival Horror:** Resources scarce, enemies overwhelming
**Cosmic Insignificance:** Ancient powers dwarf human concerns
**Corruption:** Good people do terrible things for understandable reasons
**Sacrifice:** What price is worth paying to stop greater evil?

## DM Notes

**Pacing:** Build psychological pressure before revealing full scope
**Player Agency:** Multiple valid responses to final choice
**Consequences:** Make all decisions meaningful and costly
**NPCs:** Show different stages of corruption and coping
**Combat:** Use terrain and environment as active elements
**Investigation:** Reward clever questioning and observation
**Horror:** Focus on dread and implications over gore
